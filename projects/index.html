lethe

A prototype private messenger with no user identifiers at all: no accounts, phone numbers or usernames. Contacts talk over pairwise anonymous queues on relays (SimpleX-style), with end-to-end encryption via an X3DH-style handshake and the Double Ratchet, pinned TLS 1.3, fixed-size frames and optional Tor. Ships as a CLI and a Tauri desktop/mobile app. Work in progress and unaudited.

  • Rust
  • Double Ratchet
  • Tauri
  • Tor

PGP Messenger

A desktop app for encrypting, decrypting, signing and verifying messages and files with OpenPGP. It generates RSA keypairs, keeps a local keyring of contacts, and works alongside GnuPG, Thunderbird and ProtonMail. Built on PGPy with no hand-rolled crypto, and tested on Linux and Windows in CI.
